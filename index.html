About Us

Sri Shakti yoga

Sri Shakti Yoga is a premier yoga establishment nestled in Opaline Olympia, Navalur, Chennai, dedicated to helping individuals discover holistic wellness through the ancient practice of yoga. With a stellar rating of 4.8 out of 5, our studio has earned the trust and love of our community by offering a serene, disciplined, and nurturing environment for practitioners of all levels.

Our morning sessions, held Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM, are thoughtfully designed to energize your day and cultivate inner harmony. Whether you are a beginner stepping onto the mat for the first time or an experienced yogi deepening your practice, Sri Shakti Yoga welcomes you with open arms. Located conveniently opposite Vivira Mall, we are your neighborhood sanctuary for health, mindfulness, and personal transformation.

  • Highly rated at 4.8/5 — trusted by the Navalur community for exceptional yoga instruction
